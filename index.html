Prototype rules

• Cover every residential road segment.
• Your drawn boundary is authoritative: every matching road inside it is retained.
• Cul-de-sac backtracking is accepted when unavoidable.
• No violator, complaint, or open-case data is used.
• Uses an open Chinese-Postman-style route with a flexible finish and pair-swap optimization to reduce duplicate mileage.
